# Calm storm

A dark, quiet studio rather than an everyday fundraiser template. Graphite surfaces, mist-blue type, pale product stage, and a silver contribution form create a deliberate change in light. Oversized compact Manrope headlines and restrained body copy. The real laptop leads, followed immediately by its specifications. No storm illustrations or decorative effects. Colour and proportion carry the identity. Direct bank details with a copy control and explicit transfer instructions; all prior product facts and manual confirmation constraints remain. Responsive desktop and mobile, authored SVG controls, optional paused-by-default gallery, reduced-motion support.
